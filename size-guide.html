Harness fit

Measure around the widest part of the chest, just behind the front legs.

Bed fit

Measure your dog from nose to base of tail while resting, then add approximately 15–20 cm for a relaxed sleeping footprint.

Between sizes?

For wearable items, choose the larger size and use adjustment points. For beds, choose based on the pet's preferred sleeping posture.
