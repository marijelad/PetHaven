Cat homes

Designing an indoor cat space vertically

Cats often use height to observe a room without being in the middle of its traffic. A useful indoor setup therefore includes more than one elevation and at least one protected resting spot.

Start with stable surfaces and clear landing zones. The best arrangement feels like a route rather than a collection of isolated objects.

Place one resting point near everyday activity and another in a quieter position so the cat can choose between social proximity and retreat.

A simple takeaway

Design around the pet's natural behavior first, then make the object easy to live with, clean and reposition.
